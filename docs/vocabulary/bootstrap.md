## What it means

Whatever loads before any of the real work starts: the files and skills that answer "who are we, and what state are we walking into?" Bootstrap is onboarding, not orchestration — it is not a [flow](flow.md), and it is not a [harness](harness.md).

## In this fork

`CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, memory, `/load-session-guidelines`, `/load-session-context`, and `/standup-prep` are all bootstrap in this sense. None of them do the work themselves; they establish the ground a session's real work then stands on.
